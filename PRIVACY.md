# JH Task Tracker for Anna: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

JH Task Tracker is an app on Anna AI OS, built by Jessamy Huang.

**What the app stores.** Your tasks, plans, focus logs and settings are saved in the app's own storage on Anna, linked to your Anna account. Display preferences such as theme stay in your browser.

**What I collect.** Nothing. I don't run any servers for this app, and I can't see, collect, sell or share your data.

**AI features.** When you use an AI feature, the text or images you include (for example your task list, focus notes or a meeting screenshot) are sent to the AI model through Anna. Anna's own privacy policy covers how it handles those requests.

**Deleting your data.** To delete your data, clear it in the app or delete your Anna account. Anna's policies cover what happens to app storage after uninstalling.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/JessamyH/jh-task-tracker/issues
